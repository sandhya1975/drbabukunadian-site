Privacy Policy

How information you send through this website is used.

Who we are

This website is for Dr Babu Kunadian, Consultant General and Interventional Cardiologist, who sees private patients at Nuffield Health The Grosvenor Hospital, Wrexham Road, Chester, CH4 7QP.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, phone number, email address, preferred appointment type, payment type, best time to call and any message you choose to add.
  • Review form: your first name or initials, rating and review.

Please do not include detailed medical information in either form.

How we use it

  • Enquiries are used only to contact you about arranging a consultation.
  • Reviews are checked by Dr Kunadian's secretary and published on this website only if you gave permission, using your first name or initials only.

Who receives it

Form submissions are delivered by email to Dr Kunadian's secretary, Michele White, using the FormSubmit service (formsubmit.co), which passes the message on. We do not sell or share your information for marketing.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept only as long as needed to respond to you and arrange your care. Published reviews stay on the website until you ask for them to be removed.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you can ask to see, correct or delete the information you sent us, or ask for your review to be removed. Contact Dr Kunadian's secretary on 07716 151272 or michele.white@lhch.nhs.uk.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
