Cardiology Second Opinion

If you have been given a heart diagnosis, told you need a procedure, or still don't have clear answers, an expert second opinion can give you confidence in the way forward.

Book a consultation 📞 01244 680 444

When a second opinion helps

  • You have been diagnosed with a heart condition and want reassurance
  • You have been advised to have a procedure, such as an angiogram or stent, and want to understand your options
  • Your symptoms continue despite treatment
  • Your test results are unclear, or you have been given different advice

What to bring

  • Your GP referral letter
  • Copies of clinic letters and test results (ECGs, echo reports, scan reports)
  • A list of your current medicines
  • Your questions — write them down so nothing is missed

Why Dr Kunadian

Dr Kunadian has over 13 years of experience at the Countess of Chester Hospital and Liverpool Heart & Chest Hospital, with research published in The Lancet and BMJ. He will review your history and results carefully and explain your options clearly.

Frequently asked questions

How do I arrange a second opinion?
Ask your GP for a referral letter to Dr Kunadian, then call to book.
Can I have a second opinion by video?
Yes. Dr Kunadian offers video and telephone consultations as well as in-person appointments.
Do I need new tests?
Not always. Dr Kunadian will review your existing results first and only recommend further tests if they will help.
