High Blood Pressure & Heart Risk

High blood pressure does not usually cause any symptoms — the only way to know is to have it checked. Accurate measurement and a full risk assessment help you take control early.

Book a consultation 📞 01244 680 444

What counts as high blood pressure?

According to the NHS, high blood pressure is 140/90 or higher when checked by a healthcare professional, or 135/85 or higher when checked at home.

24-hour blood pressure monitoring (ABPM)

To confirm a diagnosis, you may be asked to wear a portable monitor that takes regular readings over 24 hours while you go about your normal day, or to take readings at home over a few days.

What an assessment covers

  • Accurate blood pressure measurement, including ABPM where needed
  • Checking your heart with an ECG or echocardiogram
  • Cholesterol and overall cardiovascular risk assessment
  • A clear plan for lifestyle changes and medicines, and follow-up

Frequently asked questions

Does high blood pressure cause symptoms?
The NHS says high blood pressure does not usually cause any symptoms, which is why getting it checked is important.
What is ABPM?
Ambulatory blood pressure monitoring uses a portable monitor that takes regular readings over 24 hours while you go about your normal day.
What reading is high?
140/90 or higher when checked by a healthcare professional, or 135/85 or higher at home, according to the NHS.
